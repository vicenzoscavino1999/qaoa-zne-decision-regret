SAMPLING
NumPy SeedSequence with 256-bit OS entropy; spawn groups in listed order, repetitions 0..99, then baseline and each cell/scale in listed order. PCG64DXSM. PROBABILITIES
Unchanged density-matrix kernel; depolarizing lambda=p on h/rx/rz and 2p on cx; global odd-integer folding. Require finite nonnegative probabilities, abs(sum-1)<=1e-9; normalize by sum before multinomial. Cache deterministic probabilities with chunk and full SHA256 hashes.

METRICS
float64 grid means MSE_N,MSE_Z; r=(MSE_N-MSE_Z)/MSE_N. R=(ideal[argmin]-min(ideal))/(max(ideal)-min(ideal)); d=R_Z-R_N. Positive MSE_N and D0 required.

PRIMARY
K_r=sum(r>0), K_d=sum(d>1e-12); all 100 repetitions count; ties fail. Upper-tail Binomial(100,.5) tests. Per-cell IUT=max(p_r,p_d), Holm adjustment over exactly four IUT p-values at alpha=.05. No practical-effect filter.

SECONDARY
Median r,d; d quartiles; median d in objective units; joint-positive count; individual 95% Clopper-Pearson intervals, descriptive without simultaneous coverage. Historical 10% MSE and strong-regret thresholds descriptive only.

FAILURE_POLICY
No early stopping, sample-size extension, seed replacement, metric-based exclusions or partial endpoint analysis. Technical I/O interruption permits deterministic recovery with identical scheduled streams; completed groups verified and reused. Numerical/source failure stops inference and requires documented audit/amendment, preserving all prior data.

ASSUMPTIONS
Usual independent PRNG stream/repetition model; not literal independence proof for deterministic random generators. Dependence across endpoints and H8 orders permitted by IUT/Holm.

DESIGN_SENSITIVITY
At n=100, K>=62 in both components is sufficient to pass Holm4. Component probability of K>=62 is .38219 at pi=.60, .76987 at .65, .96602 at .70; no power claim based on selected historical success rates.
