This is a new diagnostic, not a replication of the 120x120 campaign or a test of its discovery/replication endpoints. No archived results enter selection. Select the three pre-existing Phase C graphs for structural diversity (unweighted 4-cycle, weighted 4-vertex, weighted 6-vertex); choose the lowest and highest official noise rates .001 and .02, B=1024 per parameter point, all Richardson orders 1/2/3, and the unmitigated baseline. Use the original endpoint-excluded gamma in [0,2pi) and beta in [0,pi) grid with 12x12 points. Use 500 independent multinomial landscape replicates per configuration, independent across points, scales and methods; seed each configuration with deterministic_seed('bias_variance_v1',graph,p,method,20261015). This yields 24 configurations. Budget is per point, not a shared total across the grid.

Before the full run, time G3 at gamma=.731,beta=.419,p=.02 and scales 1/3/5/7, using the existing exact density kernel; record pilot.json. Proceed if projected compute is below 600 seconds. If slower, reduce grid uniformly before any full results are computed and record amendment. Do not select settings on favorable effects.

For objective C=-cut, obtain mean and second moment from each exact computational distribution. For independent scale estimators, E[C_ZNE]=sum w_j mu_j, Var(C_ZNE)=sum w_j^2 (E[C_j^2]-mu_j^2)/n_j, with the archived largest-remainder absolute-weight allocation. Report pointwise and grid-mean bias squared, variance and their sum; compare the sum to independently sampled MC MSE (report MC standard error, do not force agreement). Validate mean and second moment at grid point (2,3), p=.02, scale7, on G1 and G3 against Aer density simulation.

Decision diagnostic: report regret for the exact mean landscape and the MC selected minimum, normalized by the ideal grid range. Use row-major first argmin tie resolution throughout. Report the contrast mean, variance, empirical reversal fraction and sampled variance. This single contrast is illustrative and need not be the most dangerous competitor. MC regret means and SEs describe this small grid only; no FDR tests, endpoint extrapolation, causal attribution to the full campaign or selected positive-only reporting.

Record source hashes, software versions and elapsed time. All new artifacts remain in this folder; existing code, data and manuscripts remain unchanged.
