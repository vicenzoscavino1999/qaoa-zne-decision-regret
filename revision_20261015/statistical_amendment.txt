Statistical revision, 15 October 2026 (post-analysis amendment)

This revision does not replace or relabel the frozen Phase C/D protocol. All
original arrays and analysis outputs remain unchanged. The earlier audit has
already revealed the approximate sensitivity counts; this is explicitly a
post-analysis correction, not a newly preregistered confirmatory experiment.

For each fixed graph/noise/budget/order cell, the sampling unit is one of the
30 independent pseudorandom finite-shot repetitions. Retain all repetitions,
including zero differences. For X=r_MSE and Y=d_R, test H_X: P(X>0)<=1/2 and
H_Y: P(Y>0)<=1/2 by exact upper binomial tails at 1/2. Under either component
null, the conjunction p=max(p_X,p_Y) is super-uniform; no within-pair
independence assumption is needed. This tests two marginal majority claims,
not that both events coincide in a majority of repetitions. The joint-positive
frequency is reported descriptively. No symmetric difference distribution or
bootstrap-null calibration is assumed. Ties count as non-positive, not dropped.

Main revised inference: Holm familywise error control at alpha=.05 across
all 540 Phase D cells. The 33 cells excluded by the original detection-floor
rule remain in the family with p=1. The other 135 Phase C cells form a separate
discovery family (Holm .05 reported separately, not pooled). A data-dependent
mask that only increases p-values preserves their validity. No additional
nominal/median/bootstrap filters are applied to the revised rejection sets.

BY .05 is a dependence-robust FDR sensitivity for D; BH .05 is conditional on
independence/appropriate positive dependence, which is not established for the
shared noisy-reference design. C uses .10 for these historical-continuity FDR
sensitivities. Do not label a further outcome-filtered subset FDR-controlled.
The median 10% MSE threshold and tau_R remain descriptive effect-size checks.

Report original bootstrap/BH classifications alongside revised results.
Compute exact conditional empirical-bootstrap median tails only to isolate
the 10,000-resample numerical-resolution defect; these tails are not exact
frequentist p-values. Preserve original bootstrap exclusions by assigning1.
The original 11/12 instance stress-positive summary is descriptive for the
six-stratum design. Its homogeneous-binomial p-value is not used to infer a
population-average frequency across heterogeneous strata.

Primary result families and alpha choices above are fixed for this revision.
No new quantum simulations are needed. Source CSV hashes, full cell tables,
method checks, and both positive and negative results must be retained.

Numerical correction identified during independent review, same revision:
The initial strict-sign calculation retained five D cells. In two of them,
some strictly positive regret differences were 2e-16 to 1e-15, reflecting
floating-point splitting of symmetry-equivalent optima. One cell's rejection
depended on these differences. The main revised regret endpoint is therefore
P(d_R > 1e-12) > 1/2 in normalized units, keeping all 30 observations and
counting values at or below tolerance as non-positive. This tolerance exceeds
observed numerical discrepancies and is not a practically important effect
threshold. It is an explicit post-analysis numerical correction, not a
predeclared property of the original campaign or an independent replication.
Report strict zero-threshold results and sensitivity at 1e-14, 1e-10, 1e-6
as well as the 1e-12 main choice. Original arrays/metrics are unchanged.
The original MSE 10% criterion remains descriptive; a directional rejection
does not imply that criterion is met, and none implies practical importance.
